2. How to Redeem Your Codes (Step-by-Step)

New to the game? Here’s how to redeem codes on the Global Server, works for PC, Mobile and PS5:

  1. Launch Genshin Impact and log into your account.
  2. Open the Paimon Menu (the icon in the top-left corner).
  3. Hit the Settings (Gear Icon) next to your username.
  4. Head over to the Account tab.
  5. Click on "Redeem Code".
  6. Paste your code and tap Exchange.

Your rewards will land right in your in-game mailbox. Don’t forget to open it and claim them before they vanish!

3. How to Avoid Expired & Fake Codes

⚠️ Heads Up: A ton of sites post codes made for the Chinese Server (CN). Those codes won’t work on Global. Always look for codes marked “Global” or “International”.

To save yourself wasted time, always check the post date of any code. If it’s more than 3 days old, chances are it’s already expired. New codes tend to pop up on Wednesdays or Fridays.

A: Sadly no. Each code only works one time per account. Trying to reuse it will throw an “Invalid” error.
