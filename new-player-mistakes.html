⚠️ Genshin Impact: 5 Huge Mistakes Every New Player Makes

Welcome to Teyvat, Traveler! Genshin Impact is such an amazing game, but its massive world and systems can feel overwhelming when you’re just starting out. I’ve seen so many new players burn through their most valuable resources before they even know what they’re doing. These are the five most common beginner mistakes — and exactly how you can steer clear of them.

1. Wasting Primogems on the Standard Banner

The mistake: Pouring your hard-earned Primogems into Wanderlust Invocation, hoping you’ll randomly pull a great 5-star.

How to fix it: Save those Primogems for limited-time character event banners. Standard banner characters are always available, but limited units can take well over a year to come back. If you just want to test your luck without spending, mess around with our Wish Simulator instead!

2. Using Primogems to Refresh Resin

The mistake: Running out of Original Resin while farming bosses or artifacts, then spending Primogems to top it back up.

How to fix it: If you’re F2P, don’t do this. Resin refills naturally over time. Those Primogems are far more valuable saved up for character pulls than for a handful of extra artifact runs.

3. Sleeping on the Battle Pass and Welkin Moon

The mistake: Assuming you have to drop tons of cash just to get good characters.

How to fix it: If you ever plan to spend a little, the $5 Welkin Moon is easily the best value in the entire game. Just don’t forget to log in each day to collect your rewards!

4. Burning Fragile Resin Too Early

The mistake: Popping Fragile Resin (each gives 60 Original Resin) at low Adventure Ranks to farm low-level domains.

How to fix it: Hold onto your Fragile Resin until you hit at least Adventure Rank 45. Starting at AR45, artifact domains guarantee a 5-star artifact drop. Using them earlier just wastes the item on weak, throwaway loot.

5. Skipping Your Daily Commissions

The mistake: Blowing off Daily Commissions because they feel repetitive and boring.

How to fix it: They only take around 10 minutes and net you 60 Primogems every single day. That adds up to roughly 1,800 Primogems each month. Want even more ways to stack up? Check out our 10,000+ Primogem Farming Guide!
