💎 Genshin Impact Primogem Farming Guide: How to Get 10,000+ Fast

I’ve been there too: a new 5-star character gets announced, and I only have 160 Primogems sitting in my account. 😭 If you’re an F2P player just like me, you need a solid farming plan. In this guide, I’ll walk you through how I stack up 10,000+ Primogems without spending a single cent.

1. 100% Exploration (The Biggest Primogem Source)

If you haven’t fully explored newer regions like Fontaine or Natlan, you’re leaving tons of Primogems on the table.

My Pro Tip: I rely on the official HoYoLAB Interactive Map instead of wandering around blindly. Focus on chests, teleport waypoints and Shrines of Depths. A single region can easily net you 3,000–5,000 Primogems once fully cleared out.

2. Clear the Spiral Abyss Every Reset Cycle

The Abyss resets on the 1st and 16th of every month. Getting all 36 stars rewards 600 Primogems each reset. If you’re struggling with Floor 12, check out my Top 5 F2P Teams Guide to build a better team setup.

3. Hangout Events & Story Quests

Every 4-star character has a Hangout Event that gives 60 Primogems. I also never put off Archon Quests or Character Story Quests. Each Story Quest gives 60 Primogems, and they often unlock new domains and bosses too.

4. Daily Commissions & Welkin Moon

Daily Commissions reward 60 Primogems per day. It only takes about 10 minutes, so don’t skip them. If you ever feel like spending a little, I think the Welkin Moon offers the best value in the game: 300 Genesis Crystals upfront, plus 90 Primogems every day for 30 days.

5. Don’t Miss Out on Redeem Codes

HoYoverse releases codes during livestreams, special events and social media milestones. I always check my October 2026 Redeem Codes page before logging in. Most codes expire within 24–48 hours!
