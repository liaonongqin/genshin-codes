🌟 Genshin Impact: Top 5 Most Underrated Characters in 2026

Let's be honest, we all chase the shiny new 5-star characters. But sometimes, the most powerful additions to your team are the ones hiding in plain sight. Whether they are 4-stars or older 5-stars, here are the top 5 most underrated characters in Genshin Impact right now that you should definitely be building.

1. Beidou (The Uncrowned Electro Archon)

Many players forget about Beidou because she is a 4-star, but her damage potential is absolutely insane. Her elemental burst (Stormbreaker) provides incredible off-field Electro application and massive AoE damage. Paired with a Hydro character, her Electro-Charged reactions can melt through groups of enemies effortlessly.

2. Jean (The Ultimate Swiss Army Knife)

With the rise of Furina, Jean has become an S-tier support practically overnight. She provides instant team-wide healing, can wield the Viridescent Venerer set to shred enemy resistances, and her skill provides strong crowd control. If you have her, don't let her sit on the bench.

3. Sucrose (The Budget Kazuha)

Sucrose is an absolute powerhouse for reaction-based teams. She shares Elemental Mastery with her teammates, shreds enemy resistances with the Viridescent Venerer set, and can group enemies together. She might not be Kazuha, but in reaction teams, she is often just as good.

4. Yaoyao (The Dendro Lifesaver)

If you are running any Dendro core teams (like Hyperbloom or Nilou Bloom), Yaoyao is incredibly valuable. She provides constant off-field Dendro application and healing just by throwing her Radishes. She is an incredible F2P option that brings both survivability and damage.

5. Rosaria (The Cryo Speedster)

Rosaria is often overshadowed by other Cryo characters, but she provides a flat Crit Rate buff to her teammates, applies consistent Cryo off-field, and can move incredibly fast at night. If you love Freeze teams or Physical DPS, she is a fantastic choice.
